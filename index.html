Are you raising a child born outside the traditional narrative?

Or maybe you're that child, growing up knowing your story was different. Either way, you've carried questions.

The Problem

Maybe you've wondered: Is my child an accident? Does their unconventional beginning mean they're somehow less chosen? How do I help them understand their worth when the world keeps telling them their birth story doesn't fit?

Or perhaps you've asked yourself: Am I a mistake? Is there a place for me in God's design?

The Guide

I'm Nike King, and I understand this question intimately. I've asked it. I've lived it. Years ago, I knelt in prayer, overwhelmed by the weight of this very concern, when something shifted in me, a knowing that God doesn't make accidents. Only then did I realize: I needed to write this book. Not just for children who don't fit the mold, but for every parent who wonders if their child's story is still part of God's plan.

A Baby on a Mission By Nike King

The Plan

A Baby on a Mission

A collection of biblical stories, real people with unconventional beginnings. Moses grew up in the palace away from his biological parents. Jephthah was born by a different woman. Jesus himself was born in unexpected circumstances. Yet each of them? Chosen. Purposed. Central to God's plan.

This book shows your child (and you) that:

  • Your birth story is not a disqualification—it's part of your testimony
  • Before the foundations of the world were laid, God already knew and loved you
  • Your circumstances don't define your destiny; your true identity is found only in Christ.

About the Author

Nike King (Abba's Daughter) is a Christian writer, storyteller, and lover of God's Word whose greatest passion is helping people see Jesus and discover the Father's heart.

She believes that true identity is not found in our circumstances, family background, successes, failures, or even our personal experiences, but in the truth of God's Word. Through Scripture and being planted in her local church Caleb Dada Ministries, the Lord has continually opened her eyes to see her own journey through the lens of redemption, grace, and purpose, revealing a God who is far kinder, more loving, and more intentional than many people realize.

Her desire is to help children and adults alike develop the right perception of God, not through human opinions or painful experiences, but through the revelation of Jesus Christ and the truth of His Word. She is passionate about reminding people that they are seen, known, loved, and chosen by God, and that every life carries divine purpose.
